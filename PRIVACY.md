# Right Shortcuts Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Right Shortcuts is a Mac app by George Khananaev. It adds the actions you choose to Finder's right-click menu.

**Right Shortcuts collects nothing.** It has no account, no analytics and no ads, and it contains no code that connects to the internet. What it knows about you stays on your Mac.

## What the app keeps on your Mac

- **Your settings.** The menu items, file templates, quick folders and custom commands you set up, and where the menu appears.
- **The folders you allowed.** macOS needs a saved permission for each one, so the app doesn't ask again after a restart.
- **The click in progress.** When you choose a menu item, the Finder extension tells the app which files or folders you had selected. That note is deleted the moment the app picks it up.
- **Crash and hang reports.** macOS gives the app reports about its own crashes. The app keeps the five newest.
- **Activity notes.** Like most Mac apps, it writes short notes to the Mac's system log: which action ran, and any error. An error message can include a file name.

All of this sits in the app's own folders inside your Library. The app sends none of it anywhere.

## Your files

Right Shortcuts works on files only in folders you allowed, and only when you choose an action. It doesn't scan or index your files. The Finder extension draws the menu and never opens a file.

## When information leaves the app

It happens only because you asked for it:

- **Copy actions** put the path, name or text you asked for on the clipboard.
- **Open in…** hands the selected items to the app you picked.
- **AirDrop** hands the selected files to macOS, which sends them to the device you choose.
- **Custom commands** are scripts you wrote. They run as you, outside the app's sandbox, and do whatever you wrote them to do.
- **Export Diagnostics** saves a text file where you choose. It lists a summary of your settings, the folders you allowed, the last hour of activity notes and the stored crash reports, so it can contain file and folder names. The app doesn't send it. Sharing it is your decision.
- **The Privacy Policy and Support links** in the About pane open these pages in your web browser. From there on, [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies.

## Apple

If you get the app from the Mac App Store, Apple handles the download, and any payment, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). If you chose to share analytics with app developers in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements, Apple may show the developer usage statistics and crash reports for the app. Apple provides them without identifying you.

## Removing your data

Deleting the app leaves your settings behind. [Support](SUPPORT.md#remove-the-app-and-its-data) lists the folders to delete if you want everything gone.
